Line Rider

Draw a track, press Play, and watch Bosh ride it. Or tap the compass for the endless ride and nine ready-made maps.

Tools

  • Q Pencil — draw freehand
  • W Line — straight lines that snap to line ends
  • E Eraser — rub out lines
  • R Pan — move around; drag Bosh to move the start

Lines

  • 1 Normal — solid track
  • 2 Boost — speeds Bosh up the way you drew it
  • 3 Scenery — decoration, not solid

Lines are solid on one side only. The shaded edge is the ground side, so draw left to right to ride on top. Hold Shift or use Flip (F) to swap sides.

Playback

  • Space play / pause
  • U stop, back to the start
  • I flag — set a checkpoint; Play restarts there
  • ← → step one frame when paused

View & editing

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Right-drag or two fingers to pan
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
  • Hold Ctrl while drawing to turn off snapping

Your track saves in this browser automatically. Use the menu to save a file (it also opens on linerider.com) or copy a share link.
